28d · UART mistakes (M7 design, slice 5; milestone tests) — two USB-serial adapters on the Pico W's two UARTs. USB1 is wired right, but its terminal reads at 115200 baud where the board sends at 9600: each of the board's bits lasts twelve of the terminal's, so it reads runs of zeros and framing errors — garbage, as a real serial monitor shows; the card says why (it knows both rates). USB2 is wired TX to TX and RX to RX: nothing arrives either way, and two outputs share a wire — a warning (F-25), not a stop. On the micro:bit, uart.init() on pins its display uses is the micro:bit's own error
