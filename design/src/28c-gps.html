28c · A GPS module (M7 design, slice 5) — a NEO-6M GPS (GY-NEO6MV2) lies below the ESP32, turned so its header is up: GND TX RX VCC, points to wire — its TX to the ESP32's RX2 (GPIO 16), its RX from TX2 (GPIO 17): UART(2, baudrate=9600); VCC from 3V3 (3.3–5 V). Once a second at 9600 baud it sends what a real one does: $GPRMC, $GPVTG, $GPGGA, $GPGSA, $GPGSV, $GPGLL, each with its checksum. The GPS card sets where it is (Phnom Penh to start), whether it has a fix (searching: the sentences' position fields empty, as from a real one indoors), how many satellites, and a speed; its PPS LED blinks with a fix. micropyGPS (MIT) comes with the library pack to read the sentences
