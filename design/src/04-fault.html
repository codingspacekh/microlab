{{i:alert:s18}} LED1 burnt out

GP13 goes straight to the green LED with no resistor, so far too much current flowed. A real LED would now be dead.

Current≈ 48 mA
LED can take20 mA
Pin can give12 mA

Fix: put a 220 Ω resistor between GP13 and the LED, like the other two.
