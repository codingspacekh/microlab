Servo motor (SG90)

A small motor that turns to the angle you choose, from 0° to 180°, and holds it there. Used for robot arms, steering and pointers.

1How it works

Inside is a small motor, gears and a sensor that knows where the arm is. The board sends one pulse every 20 ms. How long the pulse lasts tells the servo the angle — the servo turns until its sensor agrees.

{{scene:servopulses}}

Each servo is a little different — if 0° or 180° buzzes, try 0.6 ms or 2.4 ms.

3Common mistakes

{{i:warn}}
Powered from 3V3The servo jitters or the board restarts. Use 5 V: VBUS on the Pico W, VIN on the ESP32, a battery pack for the micro:bit.
{{i:warn}}
No shared groundWith a battery pack, join its − to the board's GND, or the signal has nothing to be measured against.
{{i:warn}}
Forgot freq(50)At another frequency the servo won't move, or buzzes.
