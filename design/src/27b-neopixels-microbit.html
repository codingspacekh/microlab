27b · NeoPixels on the micro:bit (M7 design, slice 3) — the "Rainbow ring" example: a ring of 12 lies beside the micro:bit's GPIO board, wired straight to its header (no breadboard): DIN on P0, its 5V and GND — the GPIO board's 5V comes from its own USB. The micro:bit's neopixel is its own module: NeoPixel(pin0, 12), np[i] = (r, g, b), fill(), clear() and show() (MicroPython on the Pico W and ESP32 says write(); the micro:bit takes both), its errors as the micro:bit's — a colour above 255 is ValueError: invalid colour, an LED past the end IndexError: index out of bounds. On the micro:bit's own 3V a ring is a warning: what the micro:bit's 3V can give to its pins is a few hundred mA at most (the build takes the number from its datasheet), a ring of 12 asks up to 720 mA.
