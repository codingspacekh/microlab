35e · Leaving needs the teacher (M13 design, Rev 15, F-113) — the student pressed Esc (or F11, or closed the window). Nothing is lost by asking: Back to the exam is the main button. The teacher types their passcode and chooses what it is for — Leave full screen (the exam goes on, in a window), or End the exam on this computer (saved, kept, not handed in). A wrong passcode: Not the teacher's passcode, and each try waits longer (1 s, 2 s, 4 s … a minute). The same window lets the teacher reopen a handed-in exam (35f) or one whose name is taken (35b)
