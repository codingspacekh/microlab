Does the laptop see the Pico W at all?

The Pico W has no power light, so test the cable like this: unplug it, hold the white BOOTSEL button, plug it back in, then let go.

Did a drive called RPI-RP2 appear in Finder?

{{i:info}}Nothing appears? The cable only carries power — most phone-charging cables are like that. Try another cable, or another USB port. It appears? The cable is fine: the Pico W just needs MicroPython (next step), or it was busy — unplug and plug it in again without the button.
On a micro:bit: “Is the light on its back, by the USB socket, on?” · on an ESP32: “Is its red power light on?”
