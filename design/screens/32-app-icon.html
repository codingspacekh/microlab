32 · The app icon (M11 design, Rev 13, the teacher: "please create the app icon") — three options, each grown from the logo in the top bar (the chip, its pins, the green LED). Shown at full size (1024 px, as macOS draws it: a rounded square with its margin and soft shadow), then as small as the Dock, the taskbar and a file list show it (128, 64, 32, 16 px) — an icon must read at 16 px. The one chosen is exported by a script to every size macOS (.icns) and Windows (.ico) need, and used for the window, the Dock / taskbar, the installer and MicroLab's .mlab files

A · Chip

The logo itself, given depth: the chip, its pins, the LED glowing at its heart. Simplest; the same as the app's own top bar.

B · Breadboard

The chip set into a breadboard: rows of holes, its red and blue rails. Says "electronics" before "chip"; busier when small.

C · Circuit

A little circuit: the chip wired to an LED that lights — what students do in MicroLab, in one picture.
