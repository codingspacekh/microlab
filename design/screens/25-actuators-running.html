25 · Actuators, running (M7 design, slice 1) — an SG90 servo by its plug in three holes (S, +, −; its body off the breadboard): its arm turns to the angle of its pulses (0.5–2.5 ms every 20 ms), at a servo's speed, not at once; an L9110S stands in the breadboard and turns a TT motor through its MOTOR A screws — the arrow says which way, the rpm how fast (PWM on A-IA); a relay module switches a pump on a 4×AA pack of its own (COM from the pack's +, NO to the pump): its contact clicks to NO, its LED lights, water flows; the kit's RGB LED module. New for M7: screw terminals and a motor's or pump's tabs are points to wire, like a board's pins — a motor, a pump and (screen 25b) a stepper or an L298N lie beside the breadboard, not in it.
