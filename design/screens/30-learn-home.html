How electronics works

Every part has a page: what it is, a look inside to play with, the idea behind it, its numbers, and how to connect it. Start with the Basics — the parts in almost every circuit.

Basics

10 parts
BreadboardJoins parts without solder: which holes are connected inside.
ResistorHolds the current back. Its stripes tell you how much.
LEDA tiny light that lets current go one way only.
RGB LEDRed, green and blue in one: mix them into any colour.
Push buttonTwo metal contacts that touch while you press.
Slide switchStays where you slide it: this way, or that way.
PotentiometerA knob that slides along a track: any voltage in between.
Light sensor (LDR)Lets more current through the more light falls on it.
BuzzerA thin disc that shakes the air: that's the sound.
Battery packWhere the push comes from: volts, and how long it lasts.
