ADC — measuring a voltage

Three pins can measure a voltage: GP26, GP27, GP28. read_u16() gives 0 for 0 V up to 65535 for 3.3 V. ADC 4 is the chip's own temperature sensor.

1from machine import ADC
2knob = ADC(26) # GP26
3print(knob.read_u16()) # 0 … 65535

Watch out

Never put 5 V on a GP pin. VBUS is 5 V whenever USB is plugged in.

The Pico W's own LED is Pin("LED"), not GP25 as on the plain Pico.
