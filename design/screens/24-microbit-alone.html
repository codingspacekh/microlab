24 · The micro:bit alone (the teacher's request, 2026-11-08) — the Start screen's card and a new micro:bit project show the micro:bit on its own, as a student first holds it. Its five big rings — 0, 1, 2, 3V, GND — are its pins: a wire goes to a ring as a crocodile clip would (dashed: the rings still free), and hovering a ring says its pin, as for any pin. For the other pins (P3–P20, 5V, the I²C header) the GPIO board is first in the parts panel when the project is a micro:bit's (the micro:bit group) — dragged in or clicked, the micro:bit goes into it (screen 24b).
