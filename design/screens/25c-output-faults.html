GP15 was damaged

MOTOR1 is wired straight to GP15. A motor needs far more current than a pin can give — a real pin would now be dead, and the motor wouldn't turn.

Motor draws≈ 160 mA
Pin can give12 mA

Fix: drive it through an L9110S or L298N (Actuators): the pin to the driver's input, the motor to its screws, the driver on VBUS.
