Big idea

Voltage, current and resistance

Three words you'll meet on every part page. They're easiest to picture as water going round a loop of pipe.

1Play with it

Push (voltage)
3 V
Narrowing (resistance)
150 Ω
Flow (current): 20 mA. Push harder and more flows. Narrow the pipe and less flows. The flow is the same all the way round the loop — what goes in comes back.

2The rule

Current = push ÷ holding back. Scientists write it I = V ÷ R and call it Ohm's law. 3 V ÷ 150 Ω = 0.02 A, which is 20 mA — just right for an LED.

Glossary

VoltageHow hard the electricity is pushed. Measured in volts (V). A AA battery gives 1.5 V.
CurrentHow much electricity flows past a point each second. Amps (A), or milliamps (mA): 1000 mA = 1 A.
ResistanceHow much something holds the current back. Ohms (Ω); 1 kΩ = 1000 Ω.
CircuitA loop the current can go round, from + back to −. Break the loop and it stops.
GNDGround: the − side, where the current comes back. 0 V.
Short circuit+ joined straight to −, with nothing holding the current back. Too much flows: things get hot.
