Potentiometer

A knob that gives you any voltage in between: turn it one way for less, the other way for more. People call it a pot.

volume knobsdimmersa joystick (two of them)

1Look inside

Turn the knob
25 %
A quarter of the way round, the wiper touches the track a quarter of the way along. So leg 2 gets a quarter of the push: 0.83 V of 3.3 V. Turn it all the way and leg 2 gets all of it.

2The idea

The track is one long resistor. The wiper splits it into two parts, and the push (voltage) splits the same way: the closer the wiper is to leg 3, the more volts it picks up. This is called a voltage divider.

4Its legs and how to connect it

Leg 1one endto − (GND)
Leg 2the wiperto what reads the voltage — a board's analog pin, or the multimeter
Leg 3other endto + (3.3 V); swap 1 and 3 and the knob works backwards

5Mistakes

The wiper to + or −Turned to the end, the knob joins + straight to −: a short circuit.
Only two legs usedIt works as a variable resistor — fine, but no voltage in between to read.
