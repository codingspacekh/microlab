26 · Displays on I²C, running (M7 design, slice 2) — an SSD1306 OLED stands in the breadboard by its four pins (GND VCC SCL SDA), its screen above them: it shows what oled.text(), rect() and fill_rect() drew, pixel for pixel, when show() sends it; a 16×2 LCD with its I²C backpack lies above the breadboard — the backpack's header sticks out of its left end, so its pins are points to wire, like the L298N's: it shows the letters putstr() wrote, where move_to() put them. Both on one I²C bus (GP20 SDA, GP21 SCL, I2C0): i2c.scan() finds them at 39 (0x27) and 60 (0x3C), and a wrong address raises the board's own OSError: [Errno 5] EIO. Both on VBUS (5 V); the knob on 3V3 gives what they show. The drivers come from the library pack (ssd1306.py, i2c_lcd.py + lcd_api.py). The LCD's inspector: its address (the backpack's A0–A2 pads) and the contrast knob on its back — turned too far, the letters vanish or become boxes, as on the real one
