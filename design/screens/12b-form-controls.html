Rules for the app

Fill the width. Panels share the window by proportion between a minimum and a maximum; no fixed page width, no empty bands (N-13). Fields stretch to their column; only icons, swatches and checkboxes have a fixed size. Our controls only. Native <select>, date, time and colour inputs are refused by lint; arrow keys and typing work in every control; the system's Open / Save dialogs are the only native ones (N-14). Keyboard: Tab reaches every control; Space / Enter choose; arrows move in lists, sliders and segments.
