Some things are only **on or off**. Others can be **anything in between**. Electronics has a word for each.

## Play with it
figure: digital-analog

## The rule
**Digital has two levels; analog has every level.** A push button is [digital]: pressed or not. A potentiometer is [analog]: anywhere along its turn. To work with an analog level, a circuit measures it and turns it into a number — and the more steps it counts in, the finer it can tell two levels apart.

## Which is which
| Digital | 0 or 1 | a button, a switch, an LED that is on or off |
| Analog | any level | a knob, a light sensor, a temperature sensor |
| As a number | counted in steps | 4 steps can only say "low, lowish, highish, high"; 1024 steps can tell {supply.low} V from a little less |
