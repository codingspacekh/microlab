A breadboard joins parts for you. The secret is **strips of metal under the holes**: legs that share a strip are joined.

## Play with it
figure: breadboard-circuit

## The rule
**Same strip, same point.** Two legs in the same strip are joined, as if they were twisted together. Two legs in different strips are joined only when a part or a wire goes from one strip to the other.

## Reading a breadboard
| A column | {breadboard.strip} holes, a to e | one strip of metal; f to j, below the gap, is another |
| The gap | no metal | the top half and the bottom half of a column are not joined |
| The rails | long strips along the edges | one for +, one for −, all the way along |
