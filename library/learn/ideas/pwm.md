How do you dim a light with a switch that is only on or off? **Blink it faster than the eye can see.**

## Play with it
figure: pwm-dimming

## The rule
**On for part of the time looks like part of the brightness.** [PWM] means *pulse-width modulation*: the pulses come at a steady beat, and how wide each one is — how long it stays on — is what changes. On for half of each blink, the LED gives out half the light.

## Where you meet it
| Dimming an LED | the [duty] | on for 25 % of the time: a quarter of the light |
| A motor's speed | the same trick | on for more of the time, it turns faster |
| A buzzer's note | the [frequency] | how many pulses a second is the pitch |
