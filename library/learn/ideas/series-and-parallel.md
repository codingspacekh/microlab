There are two ways to join more than one part to a battery: **one after the other**, or **side by side**.

## Play with it
figure: series-parallel

## The rule
**In series, parts share the push. In parallel, each gets all of it.** One after the other, there is one path: the same [current] goes through every part, and the [voltage] is shared out between them. Side by side, each part has its own path and the whole voltage — and the battery has to give the current for all of them.

## Where you meet it
| In [series] | one path | an LED and its resistor; a switch and what it switches; the cells in a battery pack |
| In [parallel] | a path each | the lamps of a house: one goes out, the others stay on |
| Take one out | in series, all stop | in parallel, the rest carry on |
