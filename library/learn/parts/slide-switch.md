**A switch that stays where you put it.** Slide it one way or the other.

## Look inside
figure: switch-inside

## The idea
**One way in, a choice of two ways out.** Use only one way out and it is an on–off switch. Use both and it chooses between two things — a red light or a green one.

## Its numbers
| Legs | three in a row | the middle one is the common leg |
| Positions | two | and it stays in each |
| A finger | not needed | unlike a push button, nothing has to hold it |

## Connect it
As an on–off switch: battery + → middle leg; one outer leg → resistor → LED → battery −. The other outer leg stays empty.

## Mistakes
- **Using the two outer legs only** They are never joined to each other: nothing ever lights.
- **All three legs in one column** The breadboard joins them: the switch does nothing.
