## Findings and decisions

1. **Rotation changes holes, not the file** (no `rotation` field, `project.json` stays format 1): a part's turn is
   read from where its legs are. A turned part goes in the nearest place where its *exact* turned shape fits on free
   holes, so it keeps its legs a whole number of holes apart, as a real part. Turning four times gives the same shape
   but may end a column away from where it started (the legs' middle of a resistor is between two columns).
2. **A turned part is wired the way it is turned**: an upright resistor in one half of the breadboard has both legs in
   one strip and does nothing — as on a real breadboard. Turned across the channel (e → f) it works. The examples and
   Learn pages (M8) should say so.
3. **Wires follow the part** (*the teacher's answer at the gate, 2026-11-07: as in Tinkercad*). A hole holds one
   thing, so a wire reaches a leg through the leg's strip: a wire end in the strip of a moved or turned leg goes with
   it — the same way from the leg if that is a free hole of the leg's new strip, otherwise the nearest free hole of
   that strip (so a turned part stays wired) — and its other end stays. It stays where it is when a part that is not
   moved also has a leg in that strip (it is joined to that part too) or the strip is a rail (everyone's). No free
   hole for it: the part doesn't move (or turn), as when a leg has no room. A wire with both ends following takes its
   bends with it. No schema change: still format 1 (`moveParts` / `rotateParts` in `arrange.ts`, 8 tests).
4. **Undo covers the circuit, not the code** (the code editor has its own undo, and ⌘Z there is the editor's). The
   history lasts while the project is open (also through Learn and back) and is not saved in the file; 200 steps.
   Changes to one value within a second (a stepper held, arrows) are one step.
5. **Beyond the approved screens** (*confirmed by the teacher at the gate, 2026-11-07*): the canvas toolbar has Duplicate as well as
   screen 02's zoom, Rotate and Delete (the meter comes with M9); Undo / Redo moved to the top bar in slice 4, where
   screen 02b has them. With several selected, a note replaces the inspector.
6. **E2E runs hidden** (the teacher's request, 2026-11-06): `MICROLAB_HIDDEN=1` by default — the window is never
   shown, no Dock icon, it never takes focus; `MICROLAB_HIDDEN=0 npx playwright test` to watch.
7. **The wheel zooms** at the pointer (*the teacher's answer at the gate, 2026-11-07: as in Tinkercad*, for students
   with a mouse), and so does a pinch; **Shift + wheel pans**, as do a middle / right drag and Space + drag (a
   trackpad's two fingers now zoom too). Electron's own ⌘+ / ⌘− still zoom the whole window; the canvas uses + − 0
   without ⌘.
8. **"Everything joined" goes through wires and the board's own links, not through parts** — the pins of a push
   button are the exception (its two legs on each side are one piece of metal, so they are joined). That is the
   circuit engine's net, so the hover and the simulation always agree.
9. **Component tests may take 15 s** (was 5 s): on a busy laptop (load ~14) three of the board tests went over 5 s
   in the full parallel run with coverage, though each takes under 1 s alone. Two full runs after that passed.
10. **A bend is taken away with a double-click** (and Straighten takes them all); one is added by dragging out the
    dot in the middle of each stretch, so a click on a wire still just selects it (*kept by the teacher at the gate*).
11. **The pin card shows a pin's functions as board.json lists them** (the Pico's "PWM7B", the ESP32's
    "ADC2_CH2"); "UART" and the ESP32's "TOUCH2" are shown as they are. Learn (M8) explains them.
12. **The inspector keeps "Legs in"** (which holes) above the new Joins line (what they reach), for every part
    (*the teacher's answer at the gate*): screen 02b shows only Joins, but the holes help find a part. The header's one-line purpose ("for the red LED") and the Learn link
    wait for M8 (Learn).
13. **The catalogue is written by hand** (`src/lib/editor/api/catalogue.ts`), one line per name: the simulated
    modules take `*args` to copy the C firmware's behaviour, so their signatures are not the documented ones, and
    the explanations are for students. `tests/python/api.test.ts` keeps it honest against the simulator both ways.
14. **Found by that test: `music.ENTERTAINER` was missing from `dir(music)`** in the simulator. MicroPython's `dir()`
    lists only interned names, and it interns string literals of at most 10 characters; ENTERTAINER has 11. The
    tunes are now set with `setattr`, which interns the name, as the firmware's ROM names are.
15. **Enter waits 75 ms** after the list opens or changes before it picks (CodeMirror's guard against a key already
    on its way); the e2e test waits as a person would.
16. **Not offered yet:** `neopixel`, `dht`, `network`, `rp2`, `esp32`, `radio`, `speech` — the simulator doesn't have
    them; they come with their parts (M6, M7) and IoT (later phase), each added to the catalogue with the test.
17. **The files panel is not on an approved screen** (*kept by the teacher at the gate*): the prototype has the tabs, a
    + and a ⋯ above the code (screens 02, 07) but no drawing of the tree; it is the Board files tree (screen 15),
    opened from ⋯. Questions are asked inside the panel, as in Board files.
18. **An empty folder is not kept**: a folder exists while a file is in it — the `.mlab` format keeps files — so a
    new folder is made with its first file (`lib/helper.py`). Deleting a file can't be undone (circuit undo is
    for the circuit; the editor's undo is for typing).
19. **The micro:bit's space is "about 20 KB"** (requirements §1.12; it has no `os.statvfs` to ask): a warning from
    80 %, *won't fit* over it. Measuring it on the teacher's micro:bit would make the number exact.
20. **The recovery card is not on an approved screen** (*kept by the teacher at the gate*): screen 01 doesn't draw it;
    the architecture says the start screen offers the project back. It sits above *Start a new project*.
21. **A recovered project opens as "not saved"**, even if nothing differs from its file, so the student decides:
    Save keeps it, closing asks first. Only one project is kept (the app has one window).
22. **E2E flake fixed:** under load (the full suite) the autocomplete test's Enter once came while CodeMirror's list
    was still changing and was ignored (its 75 ms guard); the test now presses again, as a person would, and checks
    what was typed (10 / 10 alone, 45 / 45 in the full run).
