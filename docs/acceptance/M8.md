## Design

| Screen | What it shows | Approved |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Learn: home | The parts by group (Basics first; the others as their pages come), the big ideas, the boards, the glossary, a search; the top bar with Workspace and Learn only | ✅ |
| 30b The LED | What it is and what it's for; **look inside** — slide the current: the chip lights (15 mA), then burns (past 30 mA), the current's way round drawn; the idea (a one-way door, about 2 V to open); its numbers in words; how to connect it to a battery through its resistor, and how to choose the resistor (3 V 68 Ω · 5 V 220 Ω · 6 V 270 Ω · 9 V 470 Ω at 15 mA); its mistakes, as the simulator shows them; a glossary word open (*current*) | ✅ |
| 30c The potentiometer | Its cover off: **turn the knob** — the wiper slides along the carbon track; the track pulled straight shows leg 2's volts (a quarter turn of 3.3 V: 0.83 V) and the resistance each side (2.5 kΩ · 7.5 kΩ); the idea (a voltage divider); its numbers; its three legs and how to connect them; its mistakes | ✅ |
| 30d Voltage, current and resistance | An ideas page: water in a loop of pipe — the pump's push (voltage), the narrow pipe (resistance), the flow (current), with sliders; the same loop as a circuit; the rule in words, then I = V ÷ R (3 V ÷ 150 Ω = 20 mA); the glossary beside it | ✅ |
| 30e The Pico W as a part | A board page with no code: what is on it (the RP2040, the Wi-Fi chip, the USB socket, BOOTSEL, the flash memory, 40 pins), its pins by kind, its limits in words (3.3 V on a pin, 12 mA a pin, 50 mA together, 300 mA from 3V3, 5 V at VBUS, 3 ADC pins), where its power comes from, its mistakes | ✅ |

The pictures are drawn by `design/learnfigs.py` in the prototype's style; in the app each is an interactive figure, its numbers from the part's data and worked out by the simulator's own models.
