### Decisions made while building (say if any should be otherwise)

1. **Five numbered sections, not seven.** The approved pages (30b, 30c) number five: the heading says *what it is*, and *its legs* are in **Its numbers** (the LED) or with **how to connect it** (the potentiometer). The checker asks for those five; the fourth may be called *Connect it* or *Its legs and how to connect it*.
2. **The Sensors are on Learn's home and in its list from the start**, each marked *soon* until slice 8.4 — screen 30 showed them as a pill under *More parts*. The six big ideas of Draft 2 are listed the same way (screen 30 showed three).
3. **The boards are not listed yet** (Draft 2 moved their pages to later); screen 30's list had them.
4. **A right-click on a part with no page shows no menu**, rather than a menu with nothing to choose.
5. **The parts panel's way in is a second button beside the part**, shown on hover and reached by Tab — the part itself stays one button to drag.

### Found, for the teacher

1. **The LED's two pictures of a resistor disagree, as on screen 30b.** *Connect it* draws **220 Ω** on a 6 V pack (the LED's rated 20 mA), and the table under it says **6 V → 270 Ω** (15 mA) — then "Never smaller than the table says". Suggested: the picture draws the table's 270 Ω. Kept as approved until the teacher says.
2. **The potentiometer's turn**: the page says "about 300°" (30c), the picture's knob sweeps 240°, and the draft of the Basics said "a ¾ turn" (270°). A real one turns about 300°. Suggested: the picture's sweep becomes 300°.
3. **"See it happen"** beside *No resistor* (30b) is not built: it would open the simulator on a circuit that burns its LED. Is it wanted?
4. The potentiometer's link **Voltage, current, resistance →** (30c) comes with that page, in slice 8.3 — the checker refuses a link to a page that is not written.

**The teacher, 2026-11-16, after looking at slice 8.1: "all good you can commit".** The four things found above had no answer, so each stays as built: the picture's 220 Ω, the knob's sweep, no "See it happen", the ideas link with slice 8.3.
