## Findings carried in from the design

1. **The Pico and Pico W have no power light** — the helper's cable test for them is the BOOTSEL drive (requirements
   Rev 8, screen 14).
2. **macOS asks before an app may write to a removable drive** (`RPI-RP2`, `MICROBIT`): seen when the Pico W was
   updated; installing MicroPython says so first (Rev 8, screen 14b).
3. With no data on USB at all (a charge-only cable, or a board not plugged in), nothing appears in the port list *or*
   in `ioreg` — the Pico W before it was plugged in properly looked exactly like that.
